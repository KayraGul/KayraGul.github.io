Quantum Dynamics

Hamiltonians, States, and Phase-Space Evolution

\[ i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t}|\psi(t)\rangle = \hat{H}|\psi(t)\rangle \]

This page collects selected Hamiltonians used to model quantum systems and visualizes their dynamical effects on quantum states. The first example shows the Wigner function evolution of a coherent state under Kerr nonlinearity.

Kerr Nonlinearity

Kerr Hamiltonian

\[ \hat{H} = \omega\left(\hat{n}+\frac{1}{2}\right) + \chi \hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{a}\hat{a} \]

The Kerr Hamiltonian describes a nonlinear optical medium in which the phase evolution of the field depends on the photon number. Unlike the ordinary harmonic oscillator, the Kerr term introduces a photon-number-dependent energy shift.

Starting from a coherent state, this nonlinear evolution distorts the initially Gaussian-like phase-space distribution. In the Wigner representation, this produces interference structures and nonclassical features as the state evolves.

\(\omega\): oscillator frequency

\(\chi\): Kerr nonlinear coupling strength

\(\hat{a}, \hat{a}^{\dagger}\): annihilation and creation operators

\(\hat{n} = \hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{a}\): photon number operator

More Hamiltonians

Quantum Harmonic Oscillator

\[ \hat{H} = \hbar\omega \left( \hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{a} + \frac{1}{2} \right) \]

The basic model for quantized oscillations, field modes, and many foundational problems in quantum mechanics.

Driven Two-Level System

\[ \hat{H} = \frac{\hbar\omega_0}{2}\sigma_z + \hbar\Omega\cos(\omega t)\sigma_x \]

A useful model for light-matter interaction, Rabi oscillations, and qubit dynamics.

Jaynes-Cummings Model

\[ \hat{H} = \hbar\omega_c\hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{a} + \frac{\hbar\omega_q}{2}\sigma_z + \hbar g \left( \hat{a}^{\dagger}\sigma_- + \hat{a}\sigma_+ \right) \]

A central Hamiltonian for cavity quantum electrodynamics and quantum optics.
